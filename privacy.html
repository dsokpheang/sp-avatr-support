SP Avatr

Privacy policy · last updated 21 August 2026

SP Avatr does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

What the app stores on your device

Three things, all of them on your iPhone only:

  • The last local network address your vehicle's head unit answered on, so it can be found again quickly.
  • Your light/dark theme preference.
  • A cryptographic key pair, generated on your device the first time you open the app and held in the iOS Keychain, used to authenticate the connection to your own vehicle.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Network activity

The app communicates only with your vehicle's head unit, over the local Wi-Fi network your phone and your vehicle are both connected to. It does not contact any server operated by the developer or by any third party.

What we do not do

No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising. No tracking of any kind. No third-party SDKs. No accounts and no sign-in. We never receive your data, because none is ever sent.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
